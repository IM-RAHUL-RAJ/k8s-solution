What is execution quality?

When you place a limit order you set the worst price you will accept: the most you will pay on a buy, or the least you will take on a sell. The market often fills you at a better price.

Execution quality compares the price you were actually filled at with the limit you set. Green, to the right means you did better than your limit. Red, to the left means worse. A bar on the centre line means filled exactly at your limit.

Only filled orders with a limit price count. Prices are compared as the platform records them, so the comparison is most meaningful when the account and the instrument share a currency.
